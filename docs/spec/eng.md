# Spec: Engine (ENG)

## Purpose

The own physics engine under `core/engine/` (ADR 0001): math core and integrators, rigid multibody dynamics with joints
and kinematic loops, in-house collision detection, soft contact, lumped flexible members, solver health reporting and the
live loads the rest of the tool reads every tick. It runs the engine-neutral physics model handed to it by the native
adapter; translating that model, scheduling the step and every electrical path are left to PHY, KER and SIG.

Sources: "Architecture doc" is `docs/Mechatronic Simulator — Architecture and Technology Recommendation.md`;
"Requirements doc" is `docs/Mechatronic Simulator — Requirements.md`. Milestone "spike" is the calibration spike before
phase 1 (R-12), engine packages 1 and 2.

| ENG-052 | A lumped member shall carry the joints it names (load-path coupling, ruling 63): each carried body is a child of the member's parent, carried by at most one member, never a member's own body; the engine refuses otherwise. Each step it takes the carried joint's reaction (parent on child, world-aligned, including travel-limit and lock-row impulses) recorded by the previous step, or before the first step the reaction at the start state with zero accelerations (a body at rest puts its static weight on the member from the first step); its negative is the load F, M about the child origin r_c. With the member root frame (ruling 24), s = (r_c - r_root) . x, foot point p, M_p = M + (r_c - p) x F: only the in-plane transverse force F . y and the bending moment M_p . z are rerouted (the chain bends about z only, ruling 65); the axial force, the out-of-plane force and moment and the torsion stay on the parent through the joint. They go to the two load nodes around s (x = 0: the parent for a clamped root, the pin segment for a pinned one; the spring nodes (j + 1/2) l; x = L: the last segment's tip) by the lever rule w_k = (x_{k+1} - s) / l_k, and the exact opposite goes to the parent at the same points, so the pair is internal. The carried body moves with the parent and does not ride the deflected member; the engine publishes per carried joint s, F . y, M_p . z, the lower node, its weight and the member's deflection at s, and per member the largest such deflection of the step; a foot point off the span [0, L] by more than the drift threshold ends the run naming the excursion. Stability (brief M3-E7b): the lag is a negative damping of the member's bending modes when the parent can move across the member; per mode, with r = m_c (J_c phi)((J_P - J_n) phi) and x = omega h, the step's growth factor is the largest root of (1 + 2 zeta x + x^2) z^3 - (2 + r + 2 zeta x) z^2 + (1 + 2 r) z - r, and an undamped mode grows exactly when r > (1 + x^2) / 2 (r = m_c / (m_p + m_c) for one lumped node: m_c > m_p as h -> 0). The engine checks this once, at the load configuration (the first step's start state, linearised): a parent with zero inverse inertia across the member at the load is feed-forward and not checked; otherwise the decision keeps the lag's coupling between modes, the spectral radius of the whole linear step over every elastic mode and every carried joint, and refuses an undamped radius above 1 (`InvalidModelError` naming the member, the carried joint closest to its boundary and its r; ruling 67); below it the engine publishes per carried joint the inverse inertia, r and x of the mode closest to its single-mode boundary and the damped radius. Not covered by the check (ruling 68): the carried joint's travel along the member (r changes with the mode shape at the foot point; the check is at the start position only) and the moment channel (a parent free in rotation about the bending axis). Work and validity (brief M3-E7c, ruling 69 A1): per member the engine publishes the pair's net work since load, W = sum over steps of h times the pair's generalized force at the step's start times v_{n+1} (the node forces times the node displacements minus the same forces times the parent's displacement at the same points; a static load P deflecting the member by u does W = P u, twice the stored elastic energy, the rest dissipated); the largest rerouted in-plane force; the quasi-static deflection u_qs of each carried joint under the step's loads held constant (the chain's static compliance on its load nodes and its deflection under gravity, from the elastic modes at load); and the dynamic ratio, the largest carried deflection over the largest abs(u_qs) over a sliding window (brief M3-E7e, ruling 73: a running peak since load let one impulse-like load raise the base for the rest of the run). A linear member under a load applied no faster than suddenly stays below twice its quasi-static deflection (exactly, under the scheme); the member is flagged unresolved, with the time of the first such step, when the ratio exceeds 2 (1 + 0.02), the margin the chain's discretisation tolerance of ENG-022 covering geometric nonlinearity and a moving load; the flag is information, nothing is clamped. The window: a load older than it can legitimately leave only free ringing, at most 2 u_qs of that load, decaying per period of the member's first loaded mode by e^(-(pi x + 2 pi zeta)) (backward Euler's and the node damping's; x = omega_1 h, the mode with the largest share of the member's node compliance at load), so the window is N = ln(1 / 0.02) / (pi x + 2 pi zeta) periods, after which that ringing is below the margin of its size; capped at 100 periods (a choice: it binds for an undamped member at x < 0.0124; past it an old large load's ringing can raise a conservative flag), held in a fixed ring of 4 ceil(N) quarter-period buckets (floor(pi / (2 x)) steps each, at least one), published per member with the base (window_quasi_static, window_periods, window_steps; peak_quasi_static stays the largest since load). What no flag bounds: at every step the carried deflection was within 2.04 times the largest abs(u_qs) over the window before it, nothing more. Flagged without resonance (conservative, not special-cased): a single load reversal at half a period (ratio up to 4) and gravity sag opposite to the load, which shrinks the base (a sudden load against a sag of half its own deflection: ratio 3). Proven for one carried joint loaded by a force; a moment of opposite modal sign and several joints on one member are under the rule, not the bound. Carried mass (brief M3-E7d, ruling 70 b): the member's inertia carries each carried joint's mass for its own deflection motion: with G v = u'(p) the lever-weighted deflection rate at the foot point along member y relative to the parent, the generalized mass matrix gains m_c G^T G (m_c the carried body and its tree descendants; on the two nodes' transverse coordinates m_c [w_k^2, w_k w_{k+1}; w_k w_{k+1}, w_{k+1}^2]), evaluated with the mass matrix at the start-of-step configuration and held through the step; the step carries the added mass's momentum across the carriage's travel (brief M3-E7e, ruling 73): (H_n + B_n) v_{n+1} = H_n v_n + B'_{n-1} v_n + h (forces), B'_{n-1} the block at the previous step's position with today's Jacobians, so a mode's action E / omega is an adiabatic invariant and its amplitude goes as M_eff^(-1/4) with the loaded modal mass as a carriage moves (the velocity form gave M_eff^(+1/4));
