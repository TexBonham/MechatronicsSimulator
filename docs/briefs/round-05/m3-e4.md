## Deliverables

1. Joint travel limits (ENG-013; ENG-021's hard stop): per v DOF of a revolute or prismatic joint an optional lower
   and upper bound, enforced as one-sided rows in the velocity-level projected Gauss-Seidel pass with the engine's
   position stabilization, as loop closures are (ADR 0013). Zero restitution. Oracles, derived here, not from the
   implementation:
   - Rest: a mass m on a vertical prismatic joint resting on its lower limit under gravity: the limit reaction is
     m g to 1e-9 relative once settled, the coordinate stays within the stabilization tolerance of the bound (state
     the bound the scheme gives, as a function of the step), velocity exactly zero.
   - Impact: the mass arriving at speed v stops within one step (velocity after the engaging step is zero to
     1e-12), overshoot of the bound at most h v, kinetic energy never increases across any step.
   - Release: with the mass on the limit, a force F away from the limit accelerates it at (F - m g) / m from the
     first step (the row is one-sided: no holding force, which is the defect of a bilateral penalty spring).
   - Both bounds, a bound at the start coordinate, lower equal to upper (a lock: accepted, behaves as a fixed
     joint), lower above upper (rejected before stepping, ENG-049), a limit on a spherical, planar or free DOF
     (rejected with a typed error naming the joint).
   - Revolute: a pendulum falling onto a stop at angle theta_s stops there; reaction torque equals the gravity
     torque m g l sin(theta_s) to 1e-9 once settled.
   - A limit together with a joint spring and with a transmission on the same joint (the reflected row).
2. Joint friction (ENG-014, ruling 38): per v DOF a Coulomb magnitude F_f (N or N m), regularised exactly as the
   attachment friction is: an implicit damping F_f / max(|v_start|, v_s) with v_s = `friction_slip_threshold`,
   frozen at the start-of-step rate. Oracles:
   - Sliding: a mass on a horizontal prismatic joint with initial speed v0 decelerates at F_f / m; it reaches the
     slip threshold at t = m (v0 - v_s) / F_f; assert the stopping time and the distance v0^2 m / (2 F_f) within
     the bound you derive for the frozen-rate scheme (first order in h; state it in the row).
   - Hold with creep: loaded by a constant L < F_f the steady creep speed is exactly v_s L / F_f (the damper's
     fixed point); assert it to 1e-9 and expose it per joint as an approximation value the adapter can publish.
   - L > F_f: accelerates at (L - F_f) / m once above v_s.
   - Zero friction equals the frictionless run bitwise; negative or non-finite values rejected (ENG-050).
3. Per-step joint drive (ENG-051): `set_joint_drive(joint, dof, rest, stiffness, damping)` (name it to match the
   provider's style) replaces that DOF's spring from the next step and persists until set again; it is an input,
   not a model change, so it emits no event. Oracles: static sag q - rest = tau / K to 1e-9 under a constant
   load; steady lag D v / K when the rest moves at constant speed v; the backward-Euler envelope of ruling 21 (the
   published `scheme_decay` value for the driven mode is pi omega h with omega = sqrt(K / M_eff)); a drive on a
   joint under a transmission acts on the reflected coordinate; stiffness zero removes the spring; negative or
   non-finite values, unknown joint or DOF rejected with typed errors.
4. Bindings for the three, with value semantics and typed errors, and binding tests with the bad-argument edges.
5. Rows: ENG-013, ENG-014 (state the regularisation and the creep law), ENG-021 (hard stop half), ENG-051 (the
   per-step drive), tolerance rows for the oracles above (T4), contract and section index.

Determinism (invariant 4): no unordered iteration; the limit rows are appended in joint order. Failures are errors
(invariant 7): a limit pass that does not converge within the iteration cap ends the run (ENG-025). Style: braces
on all control flow, no ternary operators, no raw owning pointers across an API, no comments unless the why is
non-obvious.
