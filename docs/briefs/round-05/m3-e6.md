## The defect

G12 on the native adapter (`uv run --no-sync mechsim run test_cases/gantry/G12.yaml`, or the command its header
gives; physics at 10 substeps of 100 microseconds): at the emergency stop at 22.5 s, with the lift axis resting on
its lower limit, the lift rises about 0.4 m within 0.1 s. Nothing commands it: on a stop the firmware disables the
drivers, so the adapter clears the joint drives. A 0.4 m rise in 0.1 s under gravity with no drive is mechanical
energy from nowhere; the scheme is backward Euler, which can only dissipate. The elements in play on that axis: a
prismatic joint on its lower limit (one-sided velocity row, ruling 43), a lead screw transmission (compliant
spring, efficiency switch, possibly self-locking with its lock row, rulings 47, 48, 55), the shaft's drive being
cleared (`clear_joint_drive`, substep rest interpolation, ruling 49), the hook and the payload on a rope below.
