## Deliverables (every check ships with an analytical reference test within the tolerance its row states; where a
row says "not set", propose one (T4) and derive it)

1. INT-001 to INT-004: plugin interface (`IntegrityCheck`: name, tier, `parameters` model, `evaluate(run_inputs)
   -> CheckResult`), registry by entry point or module list, configuration from data (`integrity.yaml`: checks,
   parameters, tier per check, halt_on_crossing for tier 1 as ruling 4's field mirror), tiers additive (a higher tier
   adds results, never changes a lower tier's numbers: test it).
2. INT-005 strength: per member and mount, peak stress from the recorded end-loads with beam theory on the member's
   section (axial plus bending at each end, shear; plate theory for mounts if the section says so), safety factor
   against the material's process- and direction-dependent strength at the run's ambient temperature through
   `IntegrityMaterialView` (min, typ, max spreads reported). Reference: a cantilever under a known tip load against
   sigma = M c / I with the section's c and I.
3. INT-006 deflection: peak deflection per member over the run from the recorded chain node poses (or the member
   end-loads with the section's E I: say which and why), against the design's deflection target (DES-032); reference
   the closed-form cantilever and simply supported deflections.
4. INT-007 buckling: compression members' margin P_cr / P over the run with Euler's load and the member's end
   conditions (effective length factors); reference against the pinned-pinned column.
5. INT-008 fatigue: rainflow cycle counting on each member's stress history (state the algorithm), damage against
   the S-N allowable from `IntegrityMaterialView` (Miner's rule); reference: a sinusoidal stress at a known amplitude
   and count against the curve.
6. INT-010 fastener: each fastening's peak load (from the recorded constraint forces) against its grade's limit
   (catalog part rating); reference: a known static load.
7. INT-011 impact: peak loads over impact events in the run (recorded contact events or load steps) against the
   members' and mounts' limits with an impact factor from the configuration; reference: a step load.
8. INT-012 resonance screening: each flexible member's first natural frequency (from the member list's calibration
   or the section) against excitation frequencies present in the run (stepper step rate, commanded speeds: from the
   recorded signals) within a screening band from the configuration; reference: a member tuned to a known
   excitation.
9. INT-019 to INT-022: the design-load rule applied from the design file (never from code), flags for every member
   or mount below the minimum safety factor or over the deflection target, the per-case per-member table (peak
   stress, deflection, safety factor, flags) in R's `IntegrityResults` shape, written beside the run archive and
   returned through the facade with the load-case export (FAC-013: per-member peak loads as CSV and JSON for FEA,
   the milestone 4 consumer).
10. INT-036 thermal slot accepted and not evaluated; INT-037 cycle counts recorded for the wear seam.
11. Conversions of the INT and FAC-013 stubs; contracts.
