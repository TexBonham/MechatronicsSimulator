## The defect

On the native adapter the gantry's cases start with the payload hanging on its 0.3 m rope. G5 (a 1 kg payload)
records a rope tension of 6011 N at 12 ms and a peak of 26229 N; before the suite carried an evaluation start
that snapped the rope (breaking tension 5000 N), and it makes the lift's stepper lose steps and the rails and
fastenings see a blow at t = 0. A payload released on an unstretched rope gives at most about 2 m g = 20 N
(the step response of the rope's spring). Thousands of newtons mean the run starts with the rope stretched by
about a millimetre or with the payload moving: the payload's start position, the rope's rest length, the end
fitting's offset or the hook's pose do not agree, or the rope chain's nodes start out of line. The suite's 9 s
evaluation start now hides it; it must not need hiding.
