## The problem
M1-X4 made the behavioral stepper a position-commanded actuator with a restoring torque computed in the signal layer
from the previous tick's shaft angle. That spring is integrated explicitly by physics: on the gantry lift (hold
stiffness about 20 N m/rad on a reflected inertia near 7.5e-6 kg m^2, doubled when back-driven at eta 0.5) omega_n h
is about 2.3 at the 1 ms step, past the explicit limit of 2, so the lift oscillates and pulls out. G2 and G3 pass;
G1 and G4 fail their no-motion criterion only because of this.

## Ruling (coordinator, for owner confirmation at the gate)
The stepper's electromagnetic hold is a joint spring with a moving rest angle, applied by physics implicitly, not a
torque the signal layer feeds forward. The adapter interface already has mid-run `set_spring_offset` and
`set_spring_stiffness` (ADR 0010); this brief makes a per-tick drive of the same kind a first-class physics input.

1. Neutral physics input (core/kernel/physics_types.hpp, KER row, PHY row): `PhysicsInputs` gains
   `joint_drives`: per entry `{joint index, rest (rad or m), stiffness (N m/rad or N/m), damping (N m s/rad or
   N s/m), enabled}`. Empty means none. PhysicsParty sums generalized forces as today and passes drives through;
   two drives on one joint in one tick is a config error. Bindings follow.
2. MuJoCo adapter (core/physics/mujoco): a drive on a reflected shaft joint acts on the driven joint with
   `rest_d = rest * c`, `K_d = K / c^2`, `D_d = D / c^2`; on an unreflected joint directly. Implement it as a soft
   joint equality (`<equality joint joint1=... polycoef="rest 0 0 0 0">`, emitted by the translation for every shaft
   joint and every joint the design marks drivable, inactive at load) whose `eq_data[0]` is the rest each step and
   whose `solref` encodes K_d and D_d: use MuJoCo's direct form (negative `solref` = `(-stiffness, -damping)` in
   constraint-acceleration units, so divide K_d and D_d by the joint's effective inertia `m_eff`, which you take
   from `mj_fullM` or the armature plus reflected mass; document the formula) with `solimp` at its stiff end
   (0.99, 0.99, 0.001); toggle `eq_active`. Constraints are solved implicitly, so there is no omega h limit. The
   drive torque on the shaft is the constraint force `efc_force` of that equality reflected by c, and the shaft
   load torque (PHY-033) stays `tau_applied - J alpha` where `tau_applied` now includes the drive torque; publish the
   drive torque per joint in `PhysicsOutputs` (`joint_drive_torques`, one per drive) so the stepper can read it.
   Translation: `mechsim/physics/mujoco/translate.py` emits the equality elements and lists them in the report.
   Tests (physics-derived): a drive on a 1 kg mass with K = 1e6 N/m at h = 1 ms (omega h = 1) holds a 10 N load with
   sag F/K = 1e-5 m within 10 percent and does not grow over 2000 steps; the same with K = 1e8 (omega h = 10,
   explicit would blow up) stays bounded and sags F/K; a reflected lead screw (J = 5.4e-6, 10 kg, 8 mm lead) with the
   gantry hold stiffness holds a gravity load with sag tau_load / K within 10 percent over 2 s; rest angle moving at
   constant rate is tracked with the steady lag of a first-order spring-damper on the reflected inertia; disabled
   drive applies no force.
3. Stepper (core/signals stepper): publishes a joint drive on its shaft joint each tick: `rest = theta_cmd`
   (integrated steps, direction sign), `stiffness = K_hold = tau_hold * pi / (2 step_angle)` (linearized detent;
   the sinusoid is a milestone 2 refinement, note it), `damping = D_m` (placeholder parameter), `enabled` from the
   enable input and the fault scaling. Reads the drive torque physics publishes: pull-out when |theta_cmd -
   theta_shaft| > 2 step angles or |drive torque| > tau_avail(|omega|): re-synchronize theta_cmd to the shaft angle
   rounded to a step, count the missed steps, stall when it happens at zero commanded speed; stall fault scales
   tau_hold and tau_avail by the fraction; slip fault drops the fraction of commanded steps. Outputs keep their
   names and units (the published torque is the drive torque read back). SIG-033 reworded accordingly; the signal
   layer carries drives into `signals.physics_inputs`. Rotor inertia stays in physics (SIG-037). Tests as in the
   previous round, now through a physics stub that applies the drive implicitly (closed forms: sag
   tau_load / K_hold; the lift holds within 1 mm over 2 s at h = 1 ms; pull-out; disabled falls).
4. Builder: every motor shaft joint is drivable (the translation emits its equality); no design change needed beyond
   what the builder derives.
