## 1. Load cell and amplifier (SIG-014, LIB-019, LIB-021, LIB-025)

`LoadCell(inst, LoadCellParams)`: `SampledSensor`, analog output `signal` (volts). It senses the force on a payload
or a joint: `senses.payload` (the C++ reads `physics_outputs.payloads[i].force` magnitude, the weld force C added)
or `senses.joint` (the joint's generalized force along its axis). Ideal: V = sensitivity x excitation x F /
capacity, quantized to `resolution` (in force before conversion), `nonlinearity` tier adds the term
nonlinearity x (F / capacity)^2 x full-scale output. Over `overload_limit` the output saturates and a
`SignalRunError` is NOT raised (overload is the firmware's business); record the saturation as an approximation
line once per run. `LoadCellAmplifier(inst, AmplifierParams)`: `front_end` stage between sensor and pin; V_out =
clamp(gain x V_in + offset, 0, output_full_scale), first-order low-pass at `bandwidth` (y += (1 - exp(-2 pi f h))
(x - y)), then the ADC sample: counts = round(V_out / adc.reference_voltage x (2^adc.resolution_bits - 1)) from the
chip's ADC data (passed in as `AdcParams{bits, reference}` by the wiring; the gantry chip says 12 bits at 3.3 V).

Reference (derive in the tests): a 2 kg payload (F = 19.62 N) on the generic 5 kg cell (sensitivity 2e-3,
excitation 5 V): V = 2e-3 x 5 x 19.62 / 49.05 = 2.000e-3 V; gain 128 -> 0.2560 V; counts = round(0.2560 / 3.3 x
4095) = 318. Step response of the 10 Hz filter reaches 63.2 percent at t = 1 / (2 pi 10) = 15.9 ms (test at the
sample nearest 16 ms, within one sample's worth of the exponential). Saturation at `overload_limit`.

## 2. IMU (LIB-019, LIB-025, ruling 2)

`Imu(inst, ImuParams)`: six analog outputs. Senses a body (`senses.body`): specific force in the body frame
f = R^T (a - g) with a from the finite difference of the body's recorded world velocity over the layer period
(first sample: a = 0), gyro = body-frame angular velocity; ranges clamp, resolutions quantize, noise and delay from
`SampledSensor` (gyro noise separately). Reference: a body at rest reads (0, 0, +g) in a z-up body frame and zero
gyro; a body in free fall reads zero specific force exactly (the difference quotient of a linear velocity ramp is
exact); a body spinning at 2 rad/s about its z reads gyro_z = 2 within resolution. Outputs as analog volts are
not modelled: the IMU's outputs are the SI values themselves (the pin carries the number; ruling 2 placeholder).

## 3. Brake (SIG-020, LIB-027, ruling 1)

`Brake(inst, BrakeParams)`: safety stage, input `release` (digital), acts on `acts_on.joint`. Engaged when
`release` is low (if `power_off_engaged`) or the rail is off; engage and release delays in `periods_floor`. While
engaged it publishes a joint drive (PHY-034) at the joint's position when it engaged, stiffness K_b chosen as the
largest value inside the adapter's envelope K_d A0 h^2 < 400 with margin (the wiring passes the joint's A0, or
default K_b = brake_torque / (0.01 step-equivalent): state the formula and its bound in the `CLAUDE.md`), damping
2 sqrt(K_b / A0) ... keep it simple: damping = brake's `damping` parameter default 0; and reads the drive torque
back: if |torque| > brake_torque the brake slips: re-set the rest so the drive torque equals +/- brake_torque
opposing the motion (the detent scheme at saturation). Reference: load torque 0.3 N m under a 0.5 N m brake holds
(drive torque 0.3 within 1e-9, position unchanged); 0.8 N m slips at 0.5 N m constant (position advances, torque
pinned at 0.5 within 1e-6); released, no drive is published; release delay of 30 ms engages 30 periods late.
