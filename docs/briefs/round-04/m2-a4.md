## 1. Linear incremental encoder (ruling 32, SIG-025, LIB-025)

`IncrementalEncoder` accepts a prismatic coordinate: `resolution` is then counts per metre (the catalog part's
`resolution` dimension decides; the wiring passes `linear: true` with the coordinate), quadrature channels A and B
as today, `reversed` as today, counts from the joint's linear position. Tests: a carriage moving 0.1 m at 60000
counts per metre gives 6000 counts split over A and B edges as the angular case does; reversed flips the sign; a
stuck or dropped fault loses counts as today; the mixed case (angular resolution on a prismatic joint) is a
`SignalConfigError` naming the instance.

## 2. Load cell with no target (BLD-036 amendment, audit major)

`LoadCell` accepts no sensing target (neither payload, joint nor joint_reaction): it measures zero force every
sample (noise and quantization still apply), so a design with a load cell runs a case that attaches nothing without
a stand-in body. Test: output equals the zero-force conversion within resolution; with noise the mean over 20000
samples is within 4 sigma / sqrt(N). Binding: the `load_cell` key set allows none of the three target keys.
