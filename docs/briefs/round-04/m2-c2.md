## 1. Slip runaway (X3: the carriage reached 0.78 m/s on x_axis in G10's slip window; K_d A0 h^2 about 0.16)

increments by (1 - s); the stepper publishes rest = theta_p + tau(delta_p) / K_t with theta_p from the reported
shaft. Trace one tick of the loop at constant commanded speed v (steps per tick) with s = 0.3 and show where the
extra energy enters (candidates: the rest increment measured on the shaft-side rest that already contains the slip
offset, so slip_accum feeds the next increment; the apportioned read-back torque under slip; the pull-out or
loss-of-synchronism rule firing on the reported lead). Required behaviour: in steady slip the driven joint moves at
(1 - s) v, the reported shaft at v, the drive torque bounded by the static lag times K_t, no growth. Reference test
in the adapter or layer tests (whichever holds the defect) with the G10 numbers: belt c = r of the gantry's
pulley, v = 16 steps per tick, s = 0.3 over 500 ticks: driven speed within 1e-6 relative of (1 - s) v c, shaft
speed within 1e-6 of v, drive torque below K_t x one step angle throughout. Record the rule in PHY-006 or SIG-033 as
the defect dictates (R-18).

## 2. Brake slip phase (X3: catching a falling lift diverges on lift_axis; drive torque grows 2.7x per step at
K_b A0 h^2 = 100)

Derive the growth factor from the slip-phase update rest = theta_p + s T_b / K_b with theta_p = theta + omega h +
alpha h^2 (backward differences): at K_b A0 h^2 = 100 the implicit drive's apparent inertia and the extrapolated
